# Business Problem

## 1. Problem Statement

Quick-commerce businesses generate large volumes of data across sales, customers, products, delivery operations, inventory, marketing, and customer feedback.

When these data sources are analyzed separately, it becomes difficult to obtain a unified view of business performance and identify areas that require attention.

This project addresses this problem by developing an interactive Power BI dashboard that integrates multiple business datasets into a single analytical solution.

---

## 2. Business Objective

The primary objective is to provide a centralized dashboard that enables stakeholders to monitor business performance and analyze operational activities through interactive visualizations and KPIs.

The dashboard is designed to help answer the following business questions:

### Sales & Revenue

- What is the total revenue generated?
- How many orders are being processed?
- What is the Average Order Value (AOV)?
- How does order volume change over time?
- Which product categories contribute to revenue?

### Customer Analytics

- How many customers are placing orders?
- What is the average number of orders per customer?
- What proportion of customers are one-time versus repeat customers?
- Which products have the highest sales volume or revenue?

### Delivery Operations

- What percentage of orders are delivered on time?
- How many orders are delayed?
- What is the average delivery variance?
- How does delivery variance vary across delivery distances?
- How are orders distributed across delivery statuses?

### Inventory & Products

- How much stock is received?
- How much stock is damaged?
- What is the overall damage rate?
- What is the calculated net stock?
- How does product revenue compare with available net stock?

### Marketing Performance

- How much is being spent on marketing?
- How much revenue is generated from marketing activities?
- What is the Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)?
- What are the marketing CTR and conversion rate?
- How do impressions, clicks, and conversions progress through the marketing funnel?

### Customer Experience

- What is the average customer rating?
- How are ratings distributed across customers?
- How does customer feedback volume change over time?

---

## 3. Proposed Solution

To address these analytical requirements, the project uses Microsoft Power BI to create an interactive five-page business analytics dashboard.

The solution combines:

- Data cleaning and transformation using Power Query
- Relational data modeling
- A centralized DateTable
- DAX measures and calculated columns
- Interactive filters and cross-filtering
- KPI cards and analytical visualizations

The final solution provides a unified environment for exploring business performance across sales, customers, delivery, inventory, marketing, and customer experience.

---

## 4. Expected Business Value

The dashboard provides a structured way to:

- Monitor important business KPIs
- Analyze sales and customer behavior
- Evaluate delivery performance
- Understand inventory conditions
- Review marketing performance
- Monitor customer feedback
- Explore relationships between different business dimensions

The dashboard is intended to support data-driven analysis by making relevant business metrics accessible through an interactive reporting interface.
