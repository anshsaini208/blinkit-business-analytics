# 01 — Project Overview

## Blinkit Business Performance & Operations Analytics Dashboard

### 1.1 Project Introduction

The Blinkit Business Performance & Operations Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The project analyzes multiple areas of a quick-commerce business, including sales, customers, products, delivery operations, inventory, marketing performance, and customer feedback.

The objective is to transform raw operational data into an interactive analytical dashboard that allows users to monitor business performance and explore important business metrics through filters and cross-interactions.

---

## 1.2 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to develop a centralized analytical dashboard that provides a comprehensive view of business performance.

The dashboard focuses on answering questions such as:

- How much revenue is being generated?
- How many orders and customers are being served?
- What is the average order value?
- Which product categories contribute to revenue?
- What is the purchasing behavior of customers?
- How effectively are orders being delivered?
- How much inventory is available and how much is damaged?
- How is marketing spend performing?
- What does customer feedback and rating distribution look like?

---

## 1.3 Business Areas

The project is divided into five major analytical areas:

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of business performance using key performance indicators and overall trends.

### 2. Sales & Customer Analytics

Analyzes order trends, revenue by category, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and order value distribution.

### 3. Delivery & Operations Analytics

Analyzes delivery status, delivery variance, delayed orders, and delivery distance.

### 4. Inventory & Product Analytics

Analyzes stock levels, damaged stock, damage rate, net stock, product revenue, and category-level inventory.

### 5. Marketing & Customer Experience Analytics

Analyzes marketing spend, marketing-generated revenue, ROAS, CTR, conversion rate, customer ratings, and feedback trends.

1.8 Project Outcome
The completed dashboard provides an interactive environment for exploring business performance across multiple operational areas.
Instead of analyzing individual datasets separately, the project combines related business data into a connected Power BI model and presents the results through a consistent five-page analytical interface.
The dashboard is designed as a portfolio-level Data Analytics and Business Intelligence project demonstrating practical skills in:
- Data preparation
